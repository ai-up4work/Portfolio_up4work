# Ad-hoc Analytics Service - AI-Powered Business Intelligence Platform

**Ask Questions in Plain English, Get Instant Data Insights**

## 🚀 Project Overview

I built an intelligent business analytics platform that lets anyone ask questions about their data using everyday language - no technical skills required. The system automatically figures out what data you need, checks your permissions, and generates beautiful visualizations showing the answers.

### 🔗 Project Details
- **My Role:** Full-Stack Developer & AI Integration Specialist
- **Duration:** 6 months (Research + Development + Deployment)
- **Industry:** Electric Vehicle & Battery Swapping Operations
- **Status:** Live in Production, Serving 50+ Daily Users

---

## ❌ The Challenge

Electric vehicle companies collect massive amounts of data every day - battery health, charging patterns, customer usage, station performance, revenue, GPS tracking, and more. But getting insights from this data was incredibly difficult:

### Problems Everyone Faced:

**Business Teams Couldn't Access Data**
- Had to wait days for reports from IT teams
- Couldn't answer simple questions like "Which stations made the most money last month?"
- No way to explore data on their own
- Lost opportunities because insights came too late

**IT Teams Were Overwhelmed**
- Hundreds of data requests every week
- Had to write SQL queries manually for each request
- Same questions asked repeatedly
- No time for strategic work

**Executives Lacked Real-Time Insights**
- Couldn't make quick decisions without fresh data
- Relied on outdated weekly/monthly reports
- Missed trends and opportunities
- No way to dig deeper into numbers

**Data Was Scattered Everywhere**
- Information stored across multiple databases
- Different teams had different data sources
- Hard to connect the dots between datasets
- No single source of truth

**Security Concerns**
- Everyone couldn't see everything
- Some data was sensitive (customer info, financial details)
- No proper access control
- Risk of data breaches

**Result:** Business opportunities missed, slow decision-making, frustrated teams, and data sitting unused despite its value.

---

## ✅ The Solution I Built

An AI-powered platform where anyone can ask questions in plain English and instantly get answers with beautiful charts and insights.

### 🎯 How It Works (Simple Version)

**1. Connect Your Data**
- Link to your company's databases (no technical setup needed)
- System automatically discovers all your data
- Works with popular databases and even Excel files

**2. Ask Questions Naturally**
- Type questions like you're talking to a colleague
- "Show me top 5 performing stations this month"
- "Which batteries need replacement soon?"
- "Where are customers using our service most?"

**3. AI Understanding**
- System reads your question
- Figures out which data you need
- Checks if you have permission to see it
- Understands business context automatically

**4. Get Instant Answers**
- Beautiful charts and graphs appear in seconds
- Interactive maps for location data
- Data tables you can explore
- Export to PDF or Excel
